\textbf{Question}:\\
 The straight line $5x+4y=0$ passes through the point of intersection of the straight lines $x+2y-10 = 0$ and $2x+y+5 = 0$.
 
\textbf{Solution:}\\
The equations of the given lines are
\begin{align}
    \myvec{1 & 2}\vec{x} &= 10 \\
    \myvec{2 & 1}\vec{x} &= -5 \\
    \myvec{5 & 4}\vec{x} &= 0 
\end{align}

\text{Forming a single matrix equation, we have}
\begin{align}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 2\\
2 & 1\\
5 & 4
\end{pmatrix}
\mathbf{x} &=
\begin{pmatrix}
10\\
-5\\
0
\end{pmatrix}
\end{align}

\text{The augmented matrix is}
\begin{align}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
1 & 2 & 10\\
2 & 1 & -5\\
5 & 4 & 0
\end{array}\right)
\end{align}

\text{Applying row operations:}
\begin{align}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
1 & 2 & 10\\
2 & 1 & -5\\
5 & 4 & 0
\end{array}\right)
&\xrightarrow{R_2-2R_1,\;R_3-5R_1}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
1 & 2 & 10\\
0 & -3 & -25\\
0 & -6 & -50
\end{array}\right) \\[6pt]
&\xrightarrow{R_2/(-3)}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
1 & 2 & 10\\
0 & 1 & 25/3\\
0 & -6 & -50
\end{array}\right) \\[6pt]
&\xrightarrow{R_1-2R_2,\;R_3+6R_2}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}
1 & 0 & -20/3\\
0 & 1 & 25/3\\
0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}\right)
\end{align}

\text{Thus, the RREF gives the solution vector}
\begin{align}
\mathbf{x}=\begin{pmatrix}-\tfrac{20}{3}\\[4pt]\tfrac{25}{3}\end{pmatrix}
\end{align}

Hence, The straight line $5x+4y=0$ passes through the point of intersection of the straight lines $x+2y-10 = 0$ and $2x+y+5 = 0$.
